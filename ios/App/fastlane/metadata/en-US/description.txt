United Connect is your all-in-one resource hub for the complete United Orthopedic product portfolio. Designed specifically for United Orthopedic sales professionals, this app puts everything you need at your fingertips — whether you're in the field, in the office, or in the operating room.

Access a comprehensive library of product resources including individual product and full-range portfolio Surgical Techniques, Design Rationales, Spec Guides, IFUs, and e-Books. United Connect also provides direct access to Pricing and Contract (RFP) documents, so you always have the most current information ready when it matters most.

Key Features:
• Surgical Techniques, Design Rationales, Spec Guides, IFUs & e-Books
• Pricing and RFP Contract Documents
• Sales Tools library for fast access to sales collateral and support materials
• AI-Powered Product Knowledge Agent — get instant, accurate answers to product questions
• Downloads — save files to your United Connect Downloads folder for offline access
• User Directory — connect with colleagues and teammates
• Company Feed — stay informed with the latest updates and announcements from the United Orthopedic team

United Connect works both online and offline. Simply download the files you need to the United Connect Downloads folder and access them anytime, anywhere — even without an internet connection. Stay organized, informed, and connected with everything you need to support surgeons throughout their operative routine.
